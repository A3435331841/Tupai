Use case: ui-mockup  
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web app studio concept art  
Primary request: 为“图派 TuPai”设计准备态创作台：左侧是日常照片与 AI 推荐的方向，右侧是清楚可控的提示词、模型、画幅和生成按钮，像一间安静、可信的 AI 暗房。  
Style: warm ivory paper UI, editorial photo app, premium restrained controls, real editorial photography, crisp Chinese typography.  
Palette: #F7F1E7 paper, #211C17 ink, #BB5834 terracotta accents, #667A64 privacy success, #FFFDF8 cards.  
Composition: 16:10 desktop, straight-on, 1440 px. Top nav same as TuPai site. A four-step breadcrumb “放入照片 / 理解画面 / 选择方向 / 生成作品”, current step “选择方向” in terracotta. Main 1fr + 360 px two-column workspace. Left: large 4:5 photo of an invented young woman at a dim outdoor dinner, warm string lights, film grain; a before thumbnail and “修后预览” label; three horizontal candidate cards with Chinese summaries 自然提亮、保留夜色、雨夜霓虹; below, five small filmstrip template cards 胶片日常, 夜景提亮, 清透人像, 旅行明信片, 杂志封面. Right: sticky “当前方案” panel with Chinese summary “夜景提亮 · 温暖聚餐”, visible English prompt block, model row “Agnes 图像 2.5 Flash”, ratio row “3:4”, tiny quota “今日还可生成 8 次”, large terracotta button “生成作品”, and microcopy “原图私密 · 结果带 AI 标识”.

Constraints: all Chinese labels clean and readable; no dark-neon cyberpunk; no dense admin table; no browser chrome or device frame; no watermark; no invented logos; no gibberish; user photo clearly private. 2K high fidelity.
