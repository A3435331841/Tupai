# 社区概念图提示词

Use case: ui-mockup  
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop creative community concept art  
Primary request: 为“图派 TuPai”设计可 fork 的 AI 摄影共创社区。页面要让作品成为主角，同时让配方复用、署名顺链和“原图永不公开”一眼可见。  
Style: independent photo magazine meets curatorial community, warm ivory paper, editorial typography, premium restrained web design, real photographic works with a coherent film-like grade.  
Palette: #F7F1E7 paper, #211C17 ink, #BB5834 terracotta accent, #667A64 moss annotations, #FFFDF8 surfaces.  
Composition: 16:10 desktop, straight-on, 1440 px. Top navigation same as TuPai and selected 社区. Editorial introduction: tiny kicker “本周策展”, huge Songti title “废片，也有第二次机会。”, short line “公开作品与配方，底图始终属于你。” Right search and filter labels 发现, 共创任务, 通知, 我的作品. A tall featured story cover at upper left showing a rainy-neon portrait, title “雨夜之后”, author “小岛”, recipe chip “夜景提亮 · 雨夜霓虹”, action “做同款”. Around it an asymmetric 3-column masonry wall of seven more AI-generated photographs with varied aspect ratios: candid cafe portrait, golden mountain postcard, quiet studio portrait, black-and-white magazine cover, pet, food, seaside. Each shows minimal author and template metadata, tiny fork lineage “fork 2”. Bottom curator task band “共创主题：把秋天拍成电影” with three small covers and button “参加共创”.

Constraints: all Chinese labels clean and readable; no social-media clutter, no engagement bait, no neon cyberpunk overload, no browser chrome or device frame, no watermark, no gibberish; invented authors and faces; make privacy visible in a small green note “原图不公开”. 2K high fidelity.
